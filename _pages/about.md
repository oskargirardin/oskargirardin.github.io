Hello! I am Oskar, a PhD student at [Inria](https://www.inria.fr/fr) and [Ecole polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/) in the [RandOpt](https://team.inria.fr/randopt/) team. My supervisors are [Nikolaus Hansen](http://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~nikolaus.hansen/) and [Dimo Brockhoff](http://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~dimo.brockhoff/).

My research focuses primarily on continuous black-box (derivative-free) optimization algorithms with a focus on Evolutionary Strategies. In particular, I am working on the Covariance Matrix-Adaptation Evolution Strategy (CMA-ES).

My research interests also include (but are not limited to): Constrained and/or Noisy Optimization, Machine Learning, Reinforcement Learning and Benchmarking.
